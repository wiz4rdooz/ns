Your phone’s videos & pics as a feed – no PC needed. Nothing gets uploaded, everything stays on your phone.

Nightshift

Remove only takes files out of Nightshift – they stay on your phone. Saved files take up extra space (they’re a copy).

Delete moves gallery files to your phone’s trash (Android 11+: recoverable for 30 days). Android asks you to confirm each time. In your own folders files are deleted permanently – there’s no trash there.

Gestures: tap = pause / play · double-tap left / right = 5 s back / ahead · double-tap middle = like · swipe right = like & next, swipe left = next · hold = 2× speed (hold on the left = rewind) · tap with two fingers = hide / show the buttons (timeline and mute stay)
