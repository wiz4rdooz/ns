NIGHTSHIFT MOBILE – Nightshift on your phone, no PC needed
==========================================================

What is this?
  Nightshift as an app for iPhone and Android. You pick videos and pics right
  from your phone and watch them as a feed – even in airplane mode, without a PC.
  Your files are NEVER uploaded. They stay on your phone.

Why do I have to “upload” the app once?
  A phone can only save a web app for offline use if it was loaded once from a
  secure web address (https://…). So you upload ONLY these app files (the 6 files
  in this folder) to GitHub once, for free.
  After that the app lives on your phone and doesn’t need the internet anymore.


SETUP (one time, about 10 minutes – best done on a PC)
------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to  https://github.com  and create a free account
   (if you don’t have one yet).

2. Top right, click  “+”  →  “New repository”.
   - Repository name: e.g.  ns   (the name doesn’t matter, it just shows up in the address)
   - Select “Public” (needed for the free option – only the empty app is
     public, never your videos)
   - At the bottom, click  “Create repository”.

3. On the new page, click the link  “uploading an existing file”.
   Drag all files from this folder into the window:
     index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, icon-192.png, icon-512.png,
     apple-touch-icon.png   (you don’t need to upload this README)
   At the bottom, click  “Commit changes”.

4. At the top, click  “Settings”  →  on the left  “Pages”.
   Under “Branch”, select  main  and  / (root)  →  “Save”.
   After 1–2 minutes your address shows up at the top, e.g.:
     https://YOURNAME.github.io/ns/

5. Open this address on your phone and add it as an app:
   iPhone (Safari):   Share ↑  →  “Add to Home Screen”
   Android (Chrome):  ⋮  →  “Install app”  or  “Add to Home screen”

   Done! From now on Nightshift starts from the app icon – even without internet and without a PC.


USING IT
--------
- “Choose videos & pics”: files are saved in Nightshift and are still there the
  next time you open it. It’s a copy – it uses extra storage.
- “Just watch now”: uses no storage, just pick them again next time.
  Nightshift still remembers your likes and what you’ve already watched.
- “Choose a whole folder” (Android): if your phone supports it.
- Swipe up/down, double-tap = Like, tap = sound on / pause,
  scrub with the bar at the bottom, Autoplay and Shuffle at the top.
- Unwatched stuff comes first, then whatever you haven’t watched the longest.
- “Remove” only takes a file out of Nightshift – it stays on your phone.

Good to know
- Web apps aren’t allowed to delete or move files from your gallery.
  Deleting/moving only works in Nightshift on the PC.
- On the PC there’s still “On your phone” (QR code) – with it you can watch your
  PC videos on your phone as long as the PC is on.
- Updates: when you get a new version, just replace the files on GitHub
  (same way as step 3). The app grabs the new version
  the next time you open it with internet.
- iPhone: use Nightshift from the app icon on your Home Screen,
  not directly in Safari – that’s the only way saved videos stay around for good.
